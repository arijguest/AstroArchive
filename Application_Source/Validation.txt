AstroArchive support and diagnostics

User guide: Quick_Start.txt, or Guide in the application.
Report an issue: https://github.com/arijguest/AstroArchive/issues

Include the app/package version, exact error and relevant filename.
Repository > Diagnostics shows the last import report and performance details.
For network/live imports, include the telescope model and firmware, connection
method (automatic/manual; Seestar SMB or DWARF FTP), whether discovery succeeded,
and whether failure occurred during connection, listing, download or archive import.
For live issues, include the selected folder, polling interval/transfer limit,
whether existing captures were included and any disconnect or PC sleep.
Do not include passwords. A discovery result alone does not verify file access.
Setup log: %TEMP%\AstroArchive-setup-error.txt
Update log: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AstroArchive\updates\last-error.txt

Preview/codec support: Settings > General > Image compatibility.
Network setup and troubleshooting: Quick_Start.txt > Import over Wi-Fi / LAN.
Live imports: keep the app open and PC awake; Stop live import retains completed
imports and downloads. Do not remove RemoteDownloads while an import is running.
Windows policy blocks: check the package's signing status and Windows Security;
managed policies may require publisher approval from your administrator.
