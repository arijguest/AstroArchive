# Object catalogue attribution

AstroArchive embeds a selected-field derivative of **OpenNGC by Mattia Verga and contributors**, under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0**.

- [Original project](https://github.com/mattiaverga/OpenNGC); source files: `database_files/NGC.csv` and `database_files/addendum.csv`.
- [Licence terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.en).
- [Source acknowledgements](OpenNGC_README.md).

The derivative combines both files, keeps valid coordinates and selects Name, Type, RA, Dec, MajAx, V-Mag, M, Common names and Identifiers. Duplicate, nonexistent, individual-star, double-star and Other records are excluded. The resulting `catalog.csv` contains 12,163 objects; scientific measurements are unaltered. The derivative remains under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Catalogue matches provide candidates for an image's sky position. Ambiguous fields and uncatalogued objects may need a user-assigned target.
