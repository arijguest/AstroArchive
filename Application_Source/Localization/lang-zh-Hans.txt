The Rosette Nebula is a stellar nursery roughly 5,000 light-years away in the constellation Monoceros.	玫瑰星云是位于麒麟座的恒星摇篮，距离我们约 5000 光年。
Light from the Andromeda Galaxy has travelled for about 2.5 million years before reaching your camera.	来自仙女座星系的光要传播约 250 万年，才能到达你的相机。
Doubling your signal-to-noise ratio generally takes four times the total exposure, under similar conditions.	在相似条件下，信噪比翻倍通常需要四倍的总曝光时间。
Hydrogen-alpha light has a wavelength of about 656.3 nanometres, in the red part of the visible spectrum.	氢阿尔法（Hα）光的波长约为 656.3 纳米，位于可见光谱的红色部分。
The Pleiades are an open star cluster. Their blue nebulosity comes from dust reflecting starlight.	昴星团是一个疏散星团。其蓝色星云来自尘埃对星光的反射。
A light-year measures distance: the distance light travels in one year, about 9.46 trillion kilometres.	光年是距离单位，指光在一年内传播的距离，约为 9.46 万亿千米。
Dark frames record a sensor’s dark current and fixed-pattern signals at a matched exposure and temperature.	暗场帧在相同的曝光时间和温度下记录传感器的暗电流和固定图案信号。
Flat frames help correct vignetting and dust shadows when captured with the same optical setup.	使用相同光学配置拍摄的平场帧有助于校正暗角和灰尘阴影。
Dithering shifts the framing between exposures, helping stacking reject hot pixels and reduce pattern noise.	抖动会在每次曝光之间轻微偏移构图，有助于叠加时剔除热像素并减少图案噪声。
The Moon’s apparent width is about half a degree. Many deep-sky targets cover much more sky.	月球的视直径约为半度。许多深空目标覆盖的天区要大得多。
The stars in a constellation can be at very different distances, even when they look like neighbours.	即使看起来彼此相邻，同一星座中的恒星与我们的距离也可能相差悬殊。
The Crab Nebula, M1, is the aftermath of a supernova recorded by astronomers in 1054.	蟹状星云（M1）是一次超新星爆发的遗迹，天文学家于 1054 年记录了这次爆发。
The Ring Nebula, M57, has a three-dimensional structure: a football-shaped cloud piercing a doughnut of gas.	环状星云（M57）具有三维结构：一团橄榄球状的云穿过一个甜甜圈状的气体环。
The Dumbbell Nebula, M27, was the first planetary nebula discovered, spotted by Charles Messier in 1764.	哑铃星云（M27）是人类发现的第一个行星状星云，由查尔斯·梅西耶于 1764 年发现。
The Veil Nebula’s delicate filaments are debris from a massive star that exploded thousands of years ago.	面纱星云精致的纤维状结构，是一颗大质量恒星在数千年前爆发后留下的碎片。
The Horsehead Nebula is a dark cloud silhouetted against brighter, glowing gas behind it.	马头星云是一团暗云，在其后方更明亮的发光气体映衬下呈现出剪影。
The Sombrero Galaxy, M104, gets its hat-like appearance from a bright central bulge and a dark dust lane.	草帽星系（M104）明亮的中央核球和暗尘埃带使它看起来像一顶帽子。
The Hercules Cluster, M13, packs hundreds of thousands of stars into a ball held together by gravity.	武仙座球状星团（M13）中数十万颗恒星在引力作用下聚集成一个球体。
Omega Centauri looks like a fuzzy star to the naked eye, but contains millions of stars.	半人马座 ω 星团用肉眼看就像一颗模糊的恒星，但实际上包含数百万颗恒星。
The Cigar Galaxy, M82, is a starburst galaxy: interactions with nearby M81 fuel unusually rapid star formation.	雪茄星系（M82）是一个星暴星系：与邻近的 M81 相互作用，促使其以异常快的速度形成恒星。
The Triangulum Galaxy, M33, is the Local Group’s third-largest galaxy, after Andromeda and the Milky Way.	三角座星系（M33）是本星系群中第三大的星系，仅次于仙女座星系和银河系。
Sirius has a tiny companion, Sirius B, a white dwarf roughly the size of Earth.	天狼星有一颗很小的伴星——天狼星 B，它是一颗大小与地球相近的白矮星。
Polaris, our North Star, belongs to a triple-star system. Its brightest member is also a variable star.	我们的北极星（勾陈一）属于一个三合星系统，其中最亮的成员也是一颗变星。
Betelgeuse is a red supergiant so large that, in the Sun’s place, it would extend beyond the orbit of Mars.	参宿四是一颗红超巨星，体积之大，若放在太阳的位置，其范围将超出火星轨道。
The Double Cluster in Perseus is two neighbouring open star clusters, NGC 869 and NGC 884.	英仙座双星团由两个相邻的疏散星团 NGC 869 和 NGC 884 组成。
Jupiter spins once in about ten hours, giving the Solar System’s biggest planet its shortest day.	木星约十小时自转一周，因此这颗太阳系最大的行星拥有最短的一天。
Saturn’s rings are made mostly of water ice, with pieces ranging from tiny grains to enormous chunks.	土星环主要由水冰组成，碎块大小不一，小至微粒，大至巨块。
