AstroArchive 1.13.14
Package 1.13.14.1

- Identifying a selected Light subgroup solves one representative and updates all
  selected members. Crop, image-index and optional header differences no longer
  split a displayed subgroup into separate jobs. Failed representatives retry up
  to two other subs; a successful sample stops immediately. Stacks stay separate.
- Solved-target review visibly selects the nearest major target. Each dropdown
  shows both its catalogue ID and common name, including a target chosen via Search.
- Selected Light groups show their target's sky track across the recorded capture
  interval, including gaps and the final exposure. Stack previews retain exposure
  comments, labelled integration and saved Edited totals to recover known duration.
  Stack intervals estimated from integration are labelled; unknown gaps are not guessed.
- Below-horizon targets leave the sky area blank. The map is centred and fills the
  space left after fitting the image, with slight padding. Its reset control is
  borderless, uses a counterclockwise icon and sits close to the map edge.
- NGC2244 and Rosette Nebula labels resolve to NGC2237, grouped under Nebulae.
  Existing archive entries, imports, filenames and catalogue searches share this identity.
- Repository shows Gain beside Exposure by default, with a dash for unknown gain.
  Existing column layouts gain the heading once; later visibility choices are retained.
