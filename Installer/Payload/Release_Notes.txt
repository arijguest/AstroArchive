AstroArchive 3.1.2
Package 3.1.2.1

- Pause and resume folder, USB, network/live, Dump and edited-image imports.
  Unfinished queues survive restarts, crashes and application updates. Resume
  verifies completed archive copies, skips duplicates and restarts incomplete
  files. Resume or discard an unfinished queue from Activity.
- Download from multiple telescopes simultaneously while selecting more files.
  Each session has independent progress and Pause/Resume controls. Settings,
  filters, grouping and Analytics remain available during network imports.
- Network files appear in Import as they arrive, with transfer progress and
  numeric time estimates based on measured throughput. Live watchers show when
  they are waiting for captures or reconnecting.
- Analytics documents default to Dark, with an optional Light theme for every
  PNG, JPEG, PDF and SVG export. Charts use AstroArchive colours, a larger logo,
  and cleaner headers and footers. Export all combines the six chart options
  in one document.
