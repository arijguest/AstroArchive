AstroArchive 3.2.9
Package 3.2.9.1

One hybrid installer replaces the Offline-Setup duplicate. Setup offers Hybrid (recommended) and Offline, with an atomic per-user choice that survives repair and updates. Both modes install without internet. Existing and portable users without a recorded choice choose at first launch; Pending blocks automatic internet requests.

Preferences > General > Online access includes cache usage, a configurable limit and Clear downloaded information. Offline cancels information requests/downloads and pauses automatic update checks while retaining the separate update and plate-solver preferences. Local telescope and Stellarium connections remain available; internet actions require explicit reconnection.

A deterministic essentials pack keeps all 13,528 catalogue identities and 66,171 alias entries, all Messier/curated information, selected southern imagery, section pages and complete credits. The pack is approximately 13.54 MiB. Additional exact-identity JPL solutions, cited numerical physical measurements, verified Wikipedia introductions and reviewed Commons images are saved outside archives. Reviewed corrections/prose remain authoritative. Conditional scientific providers stay disabled without documented dataset rights.

Solar-system and comet information pages have native 3D orbit views: drag/arrows rotate, Shift-drag pans, +/− and pinch zoom, focused/Ctrl wheel zoom leaves page scrolling available. Reset, Top view, Orbit plane, Fit orbit, Inner solar system and inline expansion handle elongated paths. Complete audited elements preserve frames, epochs, time standards and retrograde orientation; missing elements show an unavailable state. Open conics have continuation arrows, clipped extents are labelled, and exaggerated markers are disclosed. Dated markers are limited to supported model/time ranges; snapshots use orbit-epoch markers. No requests run during interaction.

Setup, update review and progress windows fit the active monitor and DPI, retain scrolling bodies and visible actions, and follow app/Windows theme and language. Successful install/update/repair writes a one-time startup confirmation. Setup closes after successful launch, keeps a retry action on launch failure, and retains Finish when opening is unchecked. Rollback recovery takes precedence over success.

Archive files, target identities, ambiguity choices, existing corrections and local telescope workflows are preserved. New controls and notifications are translated into all seven interface languages.

Meteors now have an information page with a Perseid photograph, a year chart of the annual meteor showers (when each is active, how strong, and what your observing location can see) and a guide to what meteor colours mean. A meteor capture taken within a known shower's dates now shows the shower's name in Capture details.
