AstroArchive 3.2.3 — prerelease
Package 3.2.3.1

Eclipses: Sun and Moon captures taken during a solar or lunar eclipse now list under Solar Eclipse or Lunar Eclipse in the target list (files and folders are unchanged). Capture details show the eclipse kind, how much of the Sun or Moon was covered, and its magnitude; the sky map adds a close-up of both discs to scale. Coverage comes from the bundled Moon and Sun models and matches NASA eclipse magnitudes closely; solar eclipses need an observing location.

Sky maps: Sun captures get a daytime map with the Sun and Moon. Moon and planet stacks that recorded a blank pointing, lacked a clock, or used a lunar/solar capture mode now show their map, and a Moon computed below the horizon keeps its map with a hint to check the capture timezone. The map's reset button stays at the globe's corner and scales with it.

Target information: all 110 Messier objects and about 60 popular NGC, IC, Sharpless and southern targets have new in-depth overviews and imaging notes. The pane is divided into clear sections, the object ID is no longer repeated under the title, and resizing the target pane works again.

Image quality: an on-demand quality score for deep-sky subs (star width, roundness, star count and noise) and a sharpness score for planetary, lunar and solar stills, with a QUALITY column, filters and best-first ordering within sessions.

This is a prerelease for testing. Stable releases remain available separately.
