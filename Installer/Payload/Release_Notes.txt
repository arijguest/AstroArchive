AstroArchive 3.3.2
Package 3.3.2.2

Covers everything since 3.3.1.1 (first published as prerelease 3.3.2.1).

Sky map and targets
The total solar eclipse icon on the sky map now draws a corona shaped by the solar cycle and oriented to the Sun's axis. Totality captures get the object Totality, and Sun pictures taken through a non-solar telescope get the SOL filter (Totality stays VIS). All Targets has an upcoming sky events view for the next month at your observing location, with Stellarium links, computed locally.

Orbit viewer
Dates sort by time of day as well as date, and the Parameters section is hidden when it is empty.

Settings and menus
Settings has a Telescopes section to rename a saved telescope (updating its captures), change its model and camera, remove it, or recover profiles from the repository. Import now starts with Scan folder…, which replaces Scan source….

Installer
The setup window no longer shows the version line under the logo.

The guide, tours and all seven interface languages are updated for these changes.
