AstroArchive 3.1.9
Package 3.1.9.1

- AstroArchive now opens where you left it: the main window remembers its
  position, size, monitor and maximized state, including after updates. If that
  display is no longer connected, the window moves onto an available screen.
- A new startup window shows the Rosette Nebula, the installed version and
  loading progress, with astrophotography remarks, practical hints and
  celestial-object facts while your archive loads.
- Includes the 3.1.8 changes: more reliable calibration matching for master bias
  and flat frames, folder and multi-file selection for network imports, and a
  shorter Capture details panel.
