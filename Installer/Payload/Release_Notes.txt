AstroArchive 3.1.4
Package 3.1.4.1

- Refined all six Analytics charts with stronger typography, branded gradients,
  rounded metric cards and cleaner, more compact social layouts. Dark remains
  the default document theme, with a Light option across all export formats.
- Headline cards always show the whole repository's light-frame integration,
  frame count and target count, even when chart filters or continuation pages
  show only part of the data. Stacks, videos and calibrations are not double counted.
- Export PNG, JPEG, PDF or SVG in Landscape or social formats: 9:16, 4:5, 1:1,
  16:9 and 2:3. Social image presets match each layout's native pixel dimensions.
- Export animated H.264 MP4 or looping GIF charts. Export all creates one story
  across all six reports, including continuation pages. Preview animated reveals
  and Glide, Zoom or Dissolve transitions, adjust pacing and choose video size.
  No separate encoder installation is required. Progress and remaining time are
  shown; cancelling preserves previous exports and removes unfinished files.
- Dismissing a process summary hides it from both the main banner and Activity
  without stopping its work. Completed banners clear after further interaction
  or another process completes, while their Activity history remains available.
