AstroArchive 2.1.11
Package 2.1.11.1

- Import scans recognise planet names in filenames, including Jupiter and
  Saturn, and group them under Solar system. Catalogue matches take precedence;
  ambiguous names remain unresolved. Recognition also applies to Edited files.
- Connected telescope imports start directly for small selections. A compact,
  themed confirmation appears for 500 or more capture files, including files
  inside selected folders. It shows the batch size, a short summary, and
  Import / Cancel buttons. Originals remain on the telescope.
