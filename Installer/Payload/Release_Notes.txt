AstroArchive 2.1.5
Package 2.1.5.1

- Edited gives images a larger preview with a compact filename/open-image row.
  Its panel adapts to the width needed for a full-height 3:4 portrait and remembers
  manual divider adjustments during the session. Zoom, pan and Fit remain below
  the image.
- Removed the sky map, stretch selector and capture-details icon from Edited to
  maximise image space. File details remain available in the context menu.
- Help now uses consistent headings, bullet and numbered lists, highlighted
  actions and keyboard shortcuts. Search, selectable text and guide export remain
  available in both themes and at larger text sizes.
- The interactive walkthrough has shorter, structured instructions, a roomier
  dialogue and clearer Next/Finish buttons.
- Includes all changes from earlier releases, including the backup controls and
  responsive background scanning of Edited folders.
