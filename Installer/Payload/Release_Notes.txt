AstroArchive 3.2.8 prerelease
Package 3.2.8.1

Choosing or changing a capture town now applies its historical timezone to unzoned local capture clocks and updates the UTC instant used by the sky map and eclipse checks. Existing chosen-town records with stale assumed UTC are repaired when the archive opens. Explicit UTC, clock offsets and declared header time standards retain their meaning. For the Santa Ponsa eclipse example, 12 August 2026 at 20:32:54.797 CEST resolves to 18:32:54.797 UTC, placing the Sun above the western horizon.

Thumbnail credits have been audited and repaired: Commons featured-picture notices, copyright-template instructions and talk-page controls no longer appear as creator or attribution text. Real creator, collaborator, processing and licence information is preserved. The pack exporter applies the same corrections on future rebuilds.

Comet imaging notes suggest 10–30 second sub-exposures, 5–10 seconds for faster motion or longer focal lengths, and give an image-scale/motion calculation for keeping the head sharp. Entries without imaging guidance omit the imaging-notes disclosure.
