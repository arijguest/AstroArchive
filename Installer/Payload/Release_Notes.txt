AstroArchive 3.2.2 — prerelease
Package 3.2.2.1

Analytics: choose each chart's type (donut, bars or columns; the timeline stays in date order) below the chart list. Export as now shows Images & documents and Video & GIF with their formats; resolution, video timing and size sit under Export options. The AstroArchive wordmark on chart outputs is larger and centred on the logo.

Target cards in Analytics: make cards for your top 3-25 targets (by integration or light frames) or targets you choose, within the current telescope and date scope. Save one image, a ZIP of images, or an MP4/GIF slideshow at Standard, High 2x or Sharing size. Cards show a larger logo and the AstroArchive website. Choose any image of a target, including Edited images without an archive link (linked images come first), and adjust its zoom and position.

Repository, Import and Edited tables each end with a centred path bar; click it to open that folder. Settings now notes beside the Astrometry.net option that ASTAP is much quicker and recommended, with a link to set it up.

Also includes target information and analytics refinements since 3.2.1: lengthy image credits fold into a disclosure, split Serpens constellations resolve correctly, analytics previews stay visible in short windows with larger text, and saved stacks with an existing frame count recover their total exposure.

This is a prerelease for testing. Stable releases remain available separately.
