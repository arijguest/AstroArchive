AstroArchive 3.0.1
Package 3.0.1.1

- Repository > Analytics offers six branded, publication-ready charts: targets
  photographed, imaging timeline, imaging time per target, imaging time per
  telescope, filter mix and exposure lengths.
- Preview charts with date and telescope filters, a document label and optional
  rejected-light inclusion. Export PNG, JPEG, PDF or SVG. Export all combines the
  six charts into one document; PDF preserves separate pages and raster exports
  offer 150 or 300 DPI. Long rankings continue across pages without losing targets.
- Analytics use individual light frames to avoid counting stacks, videos and
  calibration frames as additional imaging time. Missing dates and exposure
  metadata are reported in the charts.

- Connect over network beside Saved telescope automatically finds Seestar and DWARF
  devices on the active local networks. Manual addresses and custom storage paths
  are available under Advanced.
- Once connected, choose individual captures or Search all. Network imports use
  the existing verified archive workflow and retain originals on the telescope.
- Large selections warn that importing over the network takes longer than USB.
- Start live import to watch the displayed folder and its subfolders. New captures
  must remain unchanged over two observations and complete a validated transfer.
  Stop keeps completed imports. Brief connection failures retry automatically.
- Advanced controls live polling, transfer limits and whether to include existing
  captures. Verified local downloads are reused on retry.
- Seestar uses direct guest SMB access without changing Windows security policies;
  DWARF uses read-only FTP. Compatible file access must be exposed by the firmware.
- The separate SMBLibrary DLL, matching source and licence notices are bundled
  with portable and installed builds.
