AstroArchive 3.1.3
Package 3.1.3.1

- Compact process summaries appear below the menu and in Activity, with progress,
  telescope details, file counts, transfer speed, elapsed time and time estimates
  where available. Completed processes retain their results and key parameters.
- Live imports stay at the top of process lists. Multiple process summaries scroll
  only when needed. Dismiss a summary without stopping its work; Show dismissed
  restores summaries and their controls in Activity.
- All Targets places videos below still images and above calibrations, including
  grouped sessions and custom column sorting.
- Search fields have rounded corners matching the other panels. The Connect over
  network button matches the height of the saved telescope dropdown.
