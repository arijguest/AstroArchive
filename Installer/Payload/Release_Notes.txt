AstroArchive 3.2.6
Package 3.2.6.1

Target information: the pane now reads from the object name and catalogue details through the overview, image, highlighted fun fact and parameters. Short image credits remain visible; long credits expand beneath the image. Sources is itself an expandable heading containing references and catalogue details.

Eclipses and capture location: solar obscuration is drawn on the sky map's Sun, with only obscured percentage in Capture details. Sun/Moon horizon checks prevent impossible local solar eclipse views. Per-file town/city choices now live in Edit metadata > Capture and refresh the saved sky/eclipses. Lunar captures omit Moon-to-target separation.

Preview reliability: missing cached FITS image descriptors/headers are recovered from the file for stacks and subframes. Very dark linear previews receive a bounded display-only brightness boost; normal previews and source pixels stay unchanged.

DSO quality: star-poor connected regions with abrupt background boundaries now contribute suspected-obstruction evidence. Trees, branches, roofs and buildings reduce quality even when stars in the clear part of the frame are sharp. Older measurements refresh on the next quality analysis.
