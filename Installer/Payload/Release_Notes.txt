AstroArchive 3.2.4 — prerelease
Package 3.2.4.1

Open in Stellarium: a new button in the preview header shows the selected capture in Stellarium, with its position and field of view plus the capture time and place when known. Nothing leaves your PC. A running Stellarium with Remote Control switched on is reused; otherwise a new Stellarium window opens.

Target information: AT A GLANCE is now PARAMETERS. Planets and the largest dwarf planets list their nominal orbit, diameter, mass and major moons; the Sun shows its diameter and mass. The Moon's information pane no longer flickers as its scrollbar appears and disappears.

Moon and lunar stacks: lunar and solar stacks from colour cameras no longer preview green, and Seestar stacks with millisecond timestamps in their names show their Stack() count again. Moon captures whose clock has no timezone, or only a date, now show the Moon on the sky map at an approximate position.

Backups and notifications: a small indicator beside the repository path shows when a backup is running, even after its notice is dismissed. The notifications menu always lists every notification, including dismissed ones, and its bell shows when a process is active.

Includes everything in 3.2.3: eclipse detection, daytime and lunar sky maps, in-depth target overviews and image-quality scoring.

This is a prerelease for testing. Stable releases remain available separately.
