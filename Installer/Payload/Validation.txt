AstroArchive support and diagnostics

User guide: Quick_Start.txt, or Guide in the application.
Report an issue: https://github.com/arijguest/AstroArchive/issues

Include the app/package version, exact error and relevant filename.
Repository > Diagnostics shows the last import report and performance details.
Setup log: %TEMP%\AstroArchive-setup-error.txt
Update log: %LOCALAPPDATA%\AstroArchive\updates\last-error.txt

Preview/codec support: Settings > Image compatibility.
Windows policy blocks: check the package's signing status and Windows Security;
managed policies may require publisher approval from your administrator.
